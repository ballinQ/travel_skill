# Safety gates

Use authoritative and current sources for hazardous activities, weather exposure, altitude, water, wildlife, driving, and local restrictions. Separate general education from individualized medical advice.

For each consequential activity define:

- Prerequisites and experience assumptions
- Required guide, permit, insurance, equipment, or transport
- Acclimatization or recovery dependency
- Weather and operational go/no-go conditions
- Symptoms or observations that require stopping or retreating
- Lower-risk fallback that preserves the trip's purpose
- Decision owner, such as licensed guide, park authority, or traveler
- Recheck time close to the activity

Do not optimize away recovery and contingency days merely to add attractions. Never portray a summit, dive, flight, wildlife encounter, or weather-dependent event as guaranteed.
