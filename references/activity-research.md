# Live attraction, ticket, tour, and guide research

Use this before recommending or ranking a bookable attraction ticket, tour, guide, day trip, transport package, or activity.

## Search inputs

Use the exact visit date or date window, party size, adult and child ages when relevant, language, pickup area, desired duration, mobility needs, and cancellation requirement. Capture whether the traveler wants admission only, skip-the-line access, a shared tour, a private guide, transport, equipment, meals, photography support, or an all-inclusive package.

Use clickable choices for missing preferences when interactive controls are available.

## Live sources

Start with the user's preferred platform. Depending on destination and inventory, check a useful mix of:

- Tripadvisor and linked experience inventory
- Expedia activities
- Trip.com attractions and tours
- Viator
- GetYourGuide
- Klook
- Attraction or authority's official ticket site
- Tour operator or licensed guide's direct site
- Relevant local marketplace with credible inventory

Do not treat this as a requirement to exhaust every platform. Search enough sources to expose meaningful differences, then verify the strongest candidates. Prefer official sources for admission rules, closures, age restrictions, entry windows, filming rules, and permit requirements.

## Match the actual product

Marketplace titles often describe similar but non-equivalent products. Before comparing prices, identify:

- Actual operator
- Exact product and route
- Admission level or ticket category
- Shared, small-group, or private format
- Duration and start time
- Meeting point, pickup zone, or hotel pickup
- Guide language and live guide versus audio guide
- Transport type
- Included admissions, equipment, meals, drinks, and fees
- Exclusions and required on-site payments
- Accessibility and minimum age or experience
- Cancellation cutoff, weather policy, and minimum-participant rule

Detect duplicate listings for the same underlying operator. Compare equivalent variants and explain mismatches.

## Live price and availability

Use the exact date, participants, currency, and variant. Record total party price and per-person price when useful, taxes and fees, displayed availability, price type, source link, and checked-at time. Do not present a starting-from price as the user's actual total.

If the requested date is not yet released, label it `inventory not open`; show the release pattern only when sourced. If live inventory is inaccessible, retain the activity concept without a price and state what must be checked later. Do not invent availability or estimate a current marketplace price.

## Review and operator quality

Keep marketplace score, review count, and review recency visible. Read recent critical and mid-score reviews for recurring problems such as skipped itinerary items, oversized groups, rushed pacing, pickup failures, guide-language mismatch, equipment quality, refund disputes, upselling, or misleading inclusions.

Verify guide licensing, operator identity, insurance, permits, or safety credentials when the activity or jurisdiction makes them material. Marketplace popularity alone is not proof of authorization or safety.

## Comparison and tradeoffs

Use aligned cards or a matrix. Normally compare:

- Official admission or self-guided baseline
- Best-value guided choice
- Most convenient choice with pickup or bundled transport
- Private, premium, or specialist upgrade when relevant

Show the exact price difference across the whole party. Align admission, skip-the-line status, guide, group size, pickup, transport, equipment, meals, duration, cancellation, rating, and review count. Give each option a concise exchange statement:

`Pay [difference] more and gain [guide/pickup/access/time]; give up [flexibility/privacy/coverage].`

Account for separately required admission or transport when the amount is verified. Do not assign invented values to convenience. Explain whether a marketplace bundle actually saves money or simply combines bookings.

Rank against the itinerary: timing, geography, fatigue, storytelling value, weather exposure, filming needs, and fallback flexibility may matter more than raw rating.

## Selection and booking state

Preserve operator, exact product variant, date/time, participants, total price, inclusions, exclusions, meeting or pickup instructions, cancellation deadline, source, and checked-at time. `Selected` is not `Booked`; change status only after user confirmation or visible booking evidence.
