# Research and verification

## Research funnel

1. Use a fast scan to identify distinct trip directions and rough feasibility.
2. Deep-research only the direction the user selects.
3. Verify bookable candidates shortly before the user makes a decision.
4. Record volatile facts with a checked-at date and a recheck point.

## Source roles

- Inspiration and social sources: discover places, visual appeal, local vocabulary, and recurring traveler sentiment.
- Official operators and authorities: verify access, schedules, closures, permits, safety, and equipment requirements.
- Booking platforms and direct providers: verify inventory, full price, room or product type, inclusions, cancellation, pickup, and payment terms.
- Reviews: identify repeated operational strengths or failures. Distinguish reviews of the actual activity or facility from reviews of an attached hotel or parent listing.

Triangulate consequential claims. A source can be useful without being authoritative for every field.

## Comparison rules

Compare like with like and normalize currency, dates, duration, taxes and fees, baggage, room type, transfers, equipment, meals, and cancellation terms. A lower headline price is not cheaper when required extras reverse the result.

Rank against the user's actual priorities. Provide:

- Best overall
- Cheapest acceptable
- Most convenient
- Comfort or experience upgrade when materially different

Explain the decisive tradeoff. Preserve one credible fallback. Avoid long undifferentiated lists.

Do not claim to have searched the entire web. Say which representative source categories were checked, when prices were observed, and that availability can change.

## Booking boundary

Research, recommendation, and filling a plan do not mark an item booked. Record a booking only from user confirmation or visible evidence. Preserve provider, exact product or room, price and currency, participants, date/time, confirmation identifier when supplied, cancellation deadline, inclusions, exclusions, pickup details, and link.

Before a purchase or reservation, surface unresolved terms that could change suitability. Follow the active environment's confirmation requirements for external actions.
