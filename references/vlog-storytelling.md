# Vlog and narration

Make filming serve the trip rather than overload it.

For each relevant activity include a compact shot sequence, filming restrictions, practical equipment notes, and one narration unit. A useful narration rhythm is:

1. What is happening in front of the traveler
2. One or two verified facts that explain why it matters
3. A surprise, tension, or contrast
4. A sincere observation that closes without forced inspiration

Prefer short spoken sentences with useful information. Distinguish legend, local interpretation, and established history. Include pronunciation or memory keywords only when helpful.

Place narration inside its corresponding date and stop on the timeline. Design alternatives for locations where tripods, drones, professional cameras, or indoor recording are restricted. Include cold-weather battery and equipment-carry considerations when relevant.
